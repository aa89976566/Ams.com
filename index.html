Build skill, improve body composition, and transform your confidence with a proven system, delivered by an elite coach with absolute accountability for your results.

Precision Boxing Technique

From the film room: progressive focus mitt combinations, pad rounds at fight pace, defensive rolling off the line, and controlled technical sparring. Every session builds ringcraft through layered combinations, head movement, and reactive footwork.

Meet Your Coach

Simon "Kingsley" Bent

World class boxing coach for competitive and recreational athletes, making the Sweet Science accessible for everyone.

With over 20 years in boxing, I'm a fully qualified and insured England Boxing Level 2 Coach, Advanced Level 3 Personal Trainer, and Strength & Conditioning Coach. I teach real boxing in a structured syllabus format, coaching clients from age 5 to 75.

Whether you're stepping into the ring for the first time or preparing for competition, my goal is simple: help you feel confident, happy, and healthy without over complicating things.
