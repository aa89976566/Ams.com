Your neighbourhood coffee shop on Brockley Road.

Fred's is a family-friendly cafe serving Allpress coffee, fresh cakes, in-house baked goods, homemade flatbread sandwiches and salads. Pull up a chair inside, or settle into the garden — free Wi‑Fi, friendly baristas, and the BBQ sausage roll regulars swear by.
