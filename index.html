Your SE4 living room.

Fred's sits at 358 Brockley Road, right opposite Crofton Park station. Neighbours come for Allpress espresso, Good & Proper matcha, Swedish flatbreads, legendary BBQ sausage rolls, and homemade cakes — then stay for free Wi‑Fi and the garden suntrap out back.
